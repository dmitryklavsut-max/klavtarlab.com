What we do

We take on work where AI has to do a real job inside a business process. Every service below is something we already run for ourselves or for a client.

  • AI agents and automation

    Agents that read your data, propose actions and carry them out within the permissions you set: publishing, replying to customers, routing orders, writing reports. Every action is logged and can require approval.

    Where it runsShopFlow: agents manage listings, reviews and orders for 21 Etsy shops.

  • AI inside your existing systems

    Language-model features added to the tools your team already uses: document and invoice processing, search over company knowledge, classification and drafting. We pick the model per task — Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or open-weight models.

    Where it runsOur own monthly accounting package for 17 shops is assembled automatically from bank and marketplace statements.

  • Private AI on your hardware

    Local models on dedicated machines for companies that can't send data to the cloud: law and accounting firms, medical practices, manufacturers. Designed around GDPR and the EU AI Act.

    Where it runsSQWADY: compact AI devices that run agents offline.

  • Web platforms and SaaS

    Multi-tenant products with accounts, roles, payments and admin panels on Next.js, Supabase and Cloudflare. We ship, then stay on to operate and develop the product.

    Where it runsShopFlow, Heartysan and Młodzik are in production and used every day.

  • Data pipelines and analytics

    Collecting, cleaning and structuring large data sets: scraping, font and markup deobfuscation, ETL, dashboards and alerts. Numbers you can make decisions on.

    Where it runsDzen Drive: 237,924 vehicle listings indexed into a curated catalogue.

  • Websites, SEO and paid growth

    Corporate websites with technical SEO, content, localisation and ad campaigns, delivered as one contract with clear stages and acceptance.

    Where it runsKirana Global Hub and Jari Jari Group, Bali, Indonesia.

How a project runs

  1. Discovery

    A call and a written brief: what the system must do, which data it touches, who approves what. You get a fixed scope and a stage plan.

  2. First working version

    Early on we ship a version that runs on your real data, so decisions are made on something you can use, not on slides.

  3. Build in stages

    Each stage has its own deliverable, acceptance and invoice. You can pause or stop after any of them.

  4. Run and improve

    We deploy, monitor and maintain. AI actions stay logged and reversible, and the system keeps learning from your feedback.
