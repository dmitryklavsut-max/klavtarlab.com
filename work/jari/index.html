The task

Jari Jari Group commissioned a full review of its online presence and a new website built on a sound technical base. The contract combines digital marketing, SEO and web development under one stage plan.

What we built

  • Full technical audit of the existing site: indexation, crawlability, speed and structure.
  • Competitor and market research, keyword research and a semantic core for the target markets.
  • SEO roadmap that turned the audit into a prioritised list of changes.
  • New website designed and built around that roadmap: UX/UI, responsive layout, page speed and deployment.

Stages of the contract

  1. Technical audit, SEO strategy and competitor analysis: indexation and crawlability, market research, keywords, semantic core and SEO roadmap
  2. Website design and development: front-end and back-end, UX/UI, responsive layout, page speed, deployment
