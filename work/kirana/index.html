The task

Kirana Global Hub works across advertising, IT services, wholesale trade and business consulting in Indonesia. The group needed one website that presents all four lines to foreign partners, ranks in search and brings enquiries through paid campaigns.

What we built

  • Corporate website with a service catalogue, a dedicated advertising section and a contact flow, built for mobile first.
  • English copy and localisation written for search and for foreign partners entering the Indonesian market.
  • Technical and on-page SEO with structured data, then a link-building programme.
  • Search and social ad campaigns with A/B-tested creatives, targeting and budget optimisation.

Stages of the contract

  1. Website development: front-end, back-end, UX/UI, responsive layout, page speed, deployment
  2. Content, copywriting and localisation
  3. On-page SEO: meta tags, headings, internal links, structured data, images
  4. Database design, data migration, indexing and performance tuning
  5. Paid search and social campaigns: creatives, targeting, A/B tests, budget optimisation
  6. Off-page SEO: backlink strategy, outreach, thematic publications
