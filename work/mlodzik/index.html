Own product: App for youth football teams

Młodzik

Animated tactics board, training tasks, player ratings and parent messages for coaches on the touchline.

The task

Coaches of children's teams explain tactics on paper, assign homework in five different chats and lose track of who did what. Kids need to see a scheme move to understand it. The app had to work on a phone or tablet right at the pitch.

What we built

  • Animated tactics board: passes, shots, dribbling, goalkeeper tips and group tactics that play step by step.
  • Calendar with tasks for every training and league match, ratings and recommendations after.
  • “Goal + assist” ranking, league table and messages for parents.
  • Clubs with their own subdomains; admin, coach and player roles.
